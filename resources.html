Resources for leaders and participants

A narrative guide, a leader guide, a participant guide, and podcasts with transcripts.

Prepared and available

Four resources, one structure

The companion resources follow the same unit structure and learning aims. Participants reading at home, facilitators preparing a gathering, and listeners revisiting a topic encounter a consistent explanation of the faith.

The narrative guide

The program’s main account of Catholic faith in a welcoming, readable voice. The five narratives develop their subjects through Scripture and authoritative sources, with concrete examples from Catholic life.

  • Five narratives, one for each unit
  • Scripture and authoritative sources
  • Concrete examples from Catholic life
  • References for continued learning
  • Connections among the units keep the whole program in view

The leader guide

Equips a priest, formation director, spiritual director, or prepared lay facilitator to lead each gathering.

  • The unit’s purpose
  • Essential readings
  • A suggested sequence
  • Discussion questions
  • Optional activities
  • Help with common misunderstandings
  • Identifies questions that call for further research or the care of a priest or trained pastoral professional

The participant guide

Key ideas, Scripture and Catechism references, room for notes, reflection questions, and invitations to practice between gatherings.

  • Key ideas
  • Scripture and Catechism references
  • Room for notes
  • Reflection questions
  • Invitations to practice between gatherings
  • Printable materials and readable digital versions — no smartphone or social media account needed

The podcast companion

Short audio episodes introduce or revisit each unit through explanation and conversation. They supplement the written materials, whether participants are preparing for a gathering or reflecting afterward.

  • Short audio episodes for each unit
  • Explanation and conversation
  • Transcripts accompany the audio
  • Source notes accompany the audio

The materials work together while allowing your parish to choose the format and pace that best fit its community.

A complete resource for your parish

The five narratives, coordinated leader and participant guides, podcasts, and supporting materials are prepared and available for use.
