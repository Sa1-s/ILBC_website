Choosing a parish format

Begin by considering which format best serves your parish: a five-Sunday preaching event, a parish retreat weekend, or a five-night parish mission. Each brings the five connected units into a setting with opportunities for discussion and participation.

Your parish can also use the materials for a deeper series with several gatherings on a particular unit, or for personal reading supported by reflection questions and audio.

Compare the formats

Preparing your parish to participate

Invite a leader or formation team to review the materials and choose a workable schedule and setting.

Leader preparation depends on the format, the group, and the facilitator’s experience. Allow enough time to read the narrative, review the leader guide, and choose the questions and activities that will serve the group.

A complete resource for your parish

The five narratives, coordinated leader and participant guides, podcasts, and supporting materials are prepared and available for use. Your parish can concentrate on bringing people together, preparing its leaders, and choosing how to make this experience part of its ongoing adult formation.

An invitation to begin

We invite your parish to make I Love Being Catholic an essential part of its adult formation. Bring together adults who want to understand their faith more fully, including those who are unsure where to begin.

Choose a format that suits your community, invite people warmly, and make room for their questions. The resources are ready to help your parish keep learning, praying, and living the faith together.
