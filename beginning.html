A beginning, not an ending

An ongoing journey of discovery, conversation, and growth in faith.

I Love Being Catholic is more than a five-week experience. It is an invitation to begin an ongoing journey of discovery, conversation, and growth in faith.
The pathway

Begin with five weeks, then go deeper

The introductory program is supported by five additional, twelve-week programs that explore its subjects more deeply. Each includes narratives, participant guides, and leader guides, providing parishes with the materials needed to continue adult formation beyond the initial five sessions.

Not just a single programI Love Being Catholic offers a continuing pathway for parish adult formation.

Additional support

Faithful teaching and thoughtful conversation

The curriculum draws on:

Sacred ScriptureThe Church’s living TraditionThe Catechism of the Catholic Church

The witness of saints and the experience of ordinary believers help illuminate how faith is lived. Those experiences are understood as the lived reception of the faith, rather than an independent source of new revelation.

Faithful teaching and thoughtful conversation belong together. Leaders are encouraged to welcome questions, use the sources provided, and recognize when a question needs individual pastoral care rather than a group answer. The purpose is to help adults grow in understanding and discipleship, not to turn every gathering into a debate or a test of religious knowledge.
